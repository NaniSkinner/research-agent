# Caller/coordinator prior-work boundary

Read prior-work.md before framing a research assignment, launching additional investigation or recommending the next test. Apply the same checkpoint when summarizing or asking follow-up questions after the researcher returns. It applies to short replies and examples as well as formal reports.

Resolve the closest existing source, artifact, story/owner and recorded outcome you can access. Pass a standalone brief with the question, exact baseline, authoritative requirements, locators for relevant prior work, established coverage and the remaining question/delta. When prior work is inaccessible, give its locator and the access limit; do not invent a conclusion or require the researcher to confirm your proposed explanation. Ask the researcher to reconcile the map before identifying something as new. The researcher must inspect sources independently; caller classifications remain claims to check.

Before conveying findings or launching a follow-up, inspect the researcher's comparison against decisive evidence. Distinguish established scope, failed attempts, existing planned work, invalidated scope, unresolved questions and unavailable evidence. If the return proposes another pass at an old approach without a meaningful difference, request the missing comparison through the supported current assignment or ask the user a bounded question. Do not automatically launch more workers or rerun checks. An unavailable result may justify a separately authorized repeat, but does not make that experiment new.

Carry verified reusable evidence and explicit unresolved deltas forward between assignments. Preserve original receipts and outputs under evidence.md. For an independent reviewer, supply the standalone question, requirements, baseline, complete candidate answer, prior-work map and primary evidence as objects to challenge; exclude author reasoning, self-assessment and previous reviewer verdicts. Use the existing fresh-review protocol. A continuing researcher may retain the assignment; a new independent review requires a fresh reviewer.

The coordinator retains existing authority boundaries. This reference does not grant implementation, live-service execution, cross-chat messaging or product approval, and does not modify installed Pstack workflows. It governs callers using this package; it does not silently change every unrelated Codex chat.

## Requested handoff document

A short handoff request triggers references/handoff.md. Carry its default contents into the assignment and inspect the document for omitted decisions, source/revision limits, existing ownership and prior outcomes. Do not require the user to restate the format. The receiving agent begins with source reconciliation and planning; document creation adds no execution or messaging authority.
